About Leo Lucky Customs

Personalized items should never feel rushed or generic.

Leo Lucky Customs started with a simple idea: every custom order represents someone’s celebration, memory, business, family, or special moment. Those details deserve care.

Our approach

We focus on thoughtful, useful custom pieces first: shirts, tumblers, bottles, totes, mugs, and gift bundles. As we grow, new products are added with purpose — not just because they can be.

Small business feel. Organized ordering experience.

Whether you need one personalized tumbler or shirts for an entire reunion, our goal is to make the ordering process simple, clear, and personal.

Before checkout, custom products ask for important details like names, text, colors, themes, design placement, sizing, and notes. That helps reduce back-and-forth and keeps your order moving smoothly.
